Green Harvester
Green Harvester is a comprehensive web platform designed to bridge the gap between sellers and customers in the agricultural market. Built with React, Node.js, MongoDB, Firebase, and Express.js, this platform enables sellers to showcase their products, customers to make purchases, and admins to oversee operations. Additionally, the platform includes a blog section for sharing valuable content with users.

Live Site
Visit the live site here: [Live Site](https://greenhar01.web.app/)


Features
Customer
Browse and purchase fresh vegetables and other products.
View product details and place orders seamlessly.
Explore blog articles for insights and tips.
Seller
Add and manage products (e.g., vegetables and other agricultural items).
Update product details as necessary.
Monitor sales and customer interactions.
Admin
Manage users (customers and sellers).
Oversee product listings and transactions.
Maintain the integrity of the platform.
Blog Section
Add and manage blogs.
Share tips, updates, and information related to agriculture and healthy living.
Technologies Used
Frontend: React.js
Backend: Node.js, Express.js
Database: MongoDB
Authentication: Firebase Authentication
Hosting: Firebase Hosting
