# Green Harvester

**Green Harvester** is a comprehensive web platform designed to bridge the gap between sellers and customers in the agricultural market. Built with **React**, **Node.js**, **MongoDB**, **Firebase**, and **Express.js**, this platform enables:  
- Sellers to showcase their products.  
- Customers to make purchases seamlessly.  
- Admins to oversee operations.  

Additionally, the platform includes a **Blog Section** for sharing valuable content related to agriculture and healthy living.  

## Live Site  
Visit the live site here: [Green Harvester](https://greenhar01.web.app/)  

---

## Features  

### Customer  
- **Browse and Purchase**: Explore fresh vegetables and other agricultural products.  
- **Detailed Product Views**: View product descriptions, pricing, and seller details.  
- **Blog Articles**: Read insights and tips about agriculture and sustainable living.  

### Seller  
- **Add Products**: Easily list vegetables and other products.  
- **Manage Inventory**: Update product details and monitor availability.  
- **Track Sales**: View customer interactions and sales history.  

### Admin  
- **User Management**: Manage accounts for both sellers and customers.  
- **Product Oversight**: Oversee product listings and ensure quality.  
- **Platform Integrity**: Monitor transactions and platform activities.  

### Blog Section  
- **Create Blogs**: Add new articles about agriculture and related topics.  
- **Manage Blogs**: Edit or delete existing blog posts.  
- **Share Knowledge**: Educate users with updates and useful tips.  

---

## Technologies Used  

- **Frontend**: React.js  
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js  
- **Database**: MongoDB  
- **Authentication**: Firebase Authentication  
- **Hosting**: Firebase Hosting
